
# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and positive environment for everyone who contributes to or interacts with this repository. All contributors, regardless of their background, experience level, gender, nationality, technical skills, or personal characteristics, are expected to behave professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behavior

Everyone participating in this project is expected to:

* Treat all contributors and community members with respect and professionalism.
* Communicate politely and constructively, even when there are disagreements.
* Be open to different ideas, opinions, approaches, and levels of experience.
* Provide constructive feedback that focuses on improving the project rather than criticizing individuals.
* Respect the work, time, and contributions of other community members.
* Report issues, bugs, and concerns responsibly.
* Follow the project's contribution guidelines and technical standards.
* Avoid inappropriate, offensive, discriminatory, or disrespectful language and behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

* Harassment, bullying, threats, or personal attacks.
* Discriminatory or offensive comments based on personal characteristics or background.
* Use of inappropriate, abusive, or offensive language.
* Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, pull requests, or other project activities.
* Publishing or sharing someone else's private or confidential information without permission.
* Trolling, insulting, or intentionally provoking other contributors.
* Any other behavior that could reasonably make the project environment unsafe, hostile, or uncomfortable for others.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainers through the appropriate communication channel. Reports will be reviewed seriously and handled respectfully and, where appropriate, confidentially.

## Consequences

Contributors who violate this Code of Conduct may receive a warning or have their comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions removed. In serious or repeated cases, maintainers may restrict or remove a person's participation in the project.

The maintainers will make decisions based on the circumstances of each situation and aim to ensure that the project remains a respectful and productive environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other project-related interactions.

## Final Note

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help create a community where everyone can learn, contribute, collaborate, and share ideas respectfully.
